## Rough India numbers, 2026

Fresher, per year, and treat these as a map rather than a measurement.

| | Services | Product | Global captive |
|---|---|---|---|
| Fresher | 3.5 to 5 L | 8 to 15 L | 18 to 30 L |
| 3 years | 6 to 9 L | 18 to 30 L | 35 to 55 L |
| Senior | 15 to 25 L | 35 to 60 L | 60 L to 1 Cr+ |

Read the columns, not the rows. **The gap across a row is bigger than the gap down a column**,
which is the same point the [company types](company-types.md) page makes with the money flow.

## Equity, briefly

You are granted shares that **vest** over four years, usually 25 percent a year, often with a
one-year cliff before anything at all.

| | |
|---|---|
| Listed company | shares have a price. Real money, just delayed |
| Startup | worth something only if it exits. **Most do not** |

!!! warning "The trap"
    "12 LPA, of which 4 is equity" at an unlisted startup is an 8 LPA offer with a lottery ticket
    attached. Value the equity at zero when comparing, and be pleasantly surprised later.

---

## Three questions worth asking

1. **What is the monthly in-hand?** Cuts through CTC entirely.
2. **How much is fixed and how much is variable?** Variable is a target, not a payment.
3. **What is the last appraisal cycle's average increment?** Tells you the next three years.

Asking these is normal and expected. Not asking is how people end up surprised in month one.

---

## Negotiating, as a fresher

You have less leverage than later, but more than zero.

- **Never state your expectation first.** Ask what the band is for the role.
- **A competing offer is the only real leverage.** One other interview changes the conversation.
- **Negotiate base, not CTC.** Base is what compounds into every future increment.
- **Get it in writing** before you resign anything.
