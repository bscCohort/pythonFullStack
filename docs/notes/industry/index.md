smaller than they look.

at all.

## The thing worth knowing early

A web developer and a machine learning engineer at the same company, on the same rung, are paid
roughly the same. **A senior engineer at a services company and a senior engineer at a product
company are not**, and the gap is large.

So the anxious question, "did I pick the right technology?", matters less than almost anyone
tells you. The path decides what your day feels like. The company decides what your career
looks like.
