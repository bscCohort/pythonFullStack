useful thing on this page.

The reason is not generosity. It is **where the money comes from**.

## The six you will meet in India

| Type | Examples | Pay | What you get |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Services** | TCS, Infosys, Wipro, Accenture | lowest | training, structure, volume hiring |
| **Product, Indian** | Zoho, Freshworks, Zerodha | good | real ownership, sane hours |
| **Startup** | anything under 50 people | low salary, equity | breadth, speed, risk |
| **Unicorn** | Swiggy, Razorpay, CRED | strong | scale problems, good engineers |
| **Global captive** | Google, Microsoft, Amazon India | highest | depth, process, prestige |
| **Consultancy** | Thoughtworks, small specialists | good | variety, high standards |

Services companies hire the most graduates by a wide margin, so **most people start in the top
row.** That is not a mistake, it is a starting point.

## What actually differs day to day

| | Services | Product |
|---|---|---|
| Who decides what to build | the client | your team |
| Codebase | changes with the project | the same one for years |
| Fixing tech debt | rarely funded | part of the job |
| On call for your own bugs | usually not | usually yes |

That last row cuts both ways. Owning your mistakes in production is stressful, and it is also
the fastest way to get good.

---

## The move that changes your pay

**Services to product, after two or three years.** You now have real experience and can clear a
product-company interview, which is where the gap closes.

!!! tip "What clears that interview"
    Not your employer's name. **A codebase you can explain end to end**, data structures and
    algorithms, and being able to say why you made a decision rather than what you did.

    You have the first one already. Say the words: SQLite to Postgres, and React unchanged,
    because the API was a contract.
